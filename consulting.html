Why Work With Me

One Consultant,
Four Lenses

Most consultants bring one lens to a case — an ABA lens, a vocational rehab lens, or a special education lens. I bring all three, plus the research background to know what's actually been shown to work.

My focus is transition: working with transition-age youth and young adults as they move from school toward independence, employment, and adulthood. That's where I've spent the last decade — building programs, running assessments, training staff, and publishing research on what makes transition planning actually work.

Whether you're a family navigating a first vocational assessment, a district building out a transition program, or an agency that needs BCBA supervision capacity, I can meet you where you are.

What I Offer

Consulting & Training
Services

BCBA-D

BCBA Supervision & ABA Consulting

Fieldwork supervision for RBTs and candidates working toward BCBA certification, plus ongoing ABA consultation for families and organizations navigating behavior support and programming.

CVE

Vocational Assessment & Training

Direct vocational assessments for individuals with disabilities, plus training for staff and organizations on how to conduct vocational assessments in-house.

CRC

Rehabilitation Counseling Supervision

Clinical supervision for rehabilitation counseling students and interns working toward CRC certification or completing practicum and fieldwork hours.

Core Focus

Transition Programming Consulting

Program design and consultation for transition-age youth and young adults — my primary area of expertise, built on 10+ years of direct programming experience.

PhD

School & District Consulting

Consultation for schools and districts on special education programming, plus staff and teacher training on assessment practices, transition programming, and related topics.

PhD

Research Consulting

Support for organizations and researchers designing studies, evaluating programs, or analyzing outcomes related to disability, transition, and workforce equity.

Also available on request: early intervention consulting. It's not a primary focus area, but I'm happy to discuss if it fits your needs.
